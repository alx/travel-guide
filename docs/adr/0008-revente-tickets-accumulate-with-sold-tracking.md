# Accumulate ticket listings with sold tracking instead of rolling window

The ReventeTicketsFR map accumulates all processed ticket listings indefinitely, with each listing carrying a `status` field (`available` / `sold`). The daily cron polls previously `available` listings for sold signals (VENDU in title, post removed, vouch comments) and flips status after Telegram confirmation. The alternative — regenerating the GeoJSON fresh each day from the last 24h of posts — was rejected because it loses the human enrichment work (venue geocoding, YouTube lookup, structured reply) the moment a listing ages out of the search window. A listing the owner spent 30 seconds structuring should stay on the map as `sold` rather than silently disappearing. Buyers use the Reddit link in the popup as the source of truth for current availability regardless.
